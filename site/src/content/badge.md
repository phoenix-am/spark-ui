## Features

- A small label for status, counts or categories, in five tones and two looks (`soft`, `solid`).
- An LED dot (`led`) that can `pulse` for things in progress.
- `render` turns it into a link or any other element.

## Accessibility

- The text carries the meaning; the LED is decorative, so status never depends on colour.

## Best practices

- Keep it to one or two words.
- Pulse only for things that are actually live, like a deploy in progress.
