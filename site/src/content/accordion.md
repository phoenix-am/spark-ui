## Features

- Stacked sections that open and close; `defaultOpen` sets which start open.
- One section at a time by default, or several with `multiple`.

## Accessibility

- Each heading is a button with `aria-expanded`; Enter or Space toggles it.
- Each panel is a labelled region, hidden from screen readers while closed.

## Best practices

- Use it to shorten long pages people scan, like FAQs and settings.
- Don't hide content everyone needs; open the most important section by default.
